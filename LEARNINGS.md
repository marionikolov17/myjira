## 25-10-2026

### System Design

Sometimes the initial design or some design/architecture/engineering decision that is chosen for the system might not be the best choice going forward but that can only be validated with time and with continuing to build and improve the system. Some things can't be fully predicted at the beginning without knowing how the development will turn out or what constraints the design might hide. Every setback acts as an experience, so future initial design and decisions are grounded in experience and some setbacks could be caught earlier and better decision could be made.
